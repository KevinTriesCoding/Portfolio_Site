My Skills

Risk Management

From dorm rooms to board rooms, my career has been defined by seeing the pothole before we drive over it. It started as a Resident Advisor, where I learned that "risk" looks like a 2 AM crisis that needs immediate de-escalation. It evolved in Revenue Cycle Management, where I audited a $480M portfolio to catch financial leaks before they became floods.

Today, as a Practice Manager, I combine those instincts with technical governance. I don't just "hope" for compliance; I engineer it. I've built Python-based monitoring tools that automatically flag "fishy" shifts and payroll discrepancies, ensuring that our operations remain compliant, our budget remains balanced, and our patients remain safe.

Stakeholder Management

I don't just manage people; I translate between them. My foundation is built on making the complex accessible. As a Content Writer at NC State, my job was to interview researchers and translate dense, technical scientific data into engaging stories for a general audience. I learned early on that if the stakeholder doesn't understand the message, the work doesn't matter.

I applied that same philosophy to Revenue Cycle Management, where I translated cryptic insurance denial codes into clear financial strategies for leadership. Now, as a Practice Manager, I serve as the central interpreter for a complex ecosystem: aligning the clinical needs of 60+ nurses, the emotional requirements of patient families, and the operational mandates of the business.

Process Improvement

Healthcare moves fast. Its tools should too. As fast-paced and fluid as the healthcare field can be, much of the work done within it is based on inefficient legacy technologies or outdated practices. I have found that even minor technology improvements can lead to sizable efficiency gains for everyone.

I don't wait for enterprise overhauls to solve these problems; I build the bridge myself. By introducing modern tools—like custom Python scripting—I've been able to automate manual billing audits and data visualization. This approach allows me to bypass the limitations of legacy software, turning hours of administrative "busy work" into instant, accurate insights for the team.

Technical Agility

I am a lifelong learner who actively hunts for the "unfair advantage." I don't fear new technology; I embrace it to give my team an edge. My philosophy is simple: if a tool can automate the mundane or accelerate the strategic, I learn it, I master it, and I implement it. I don't wait for a manual—I figure it out.

  • The Analysts (Data & Excel): From complex Excel/Sheets modeling to foundational SQL queries, I treat data as the ultimate source of truth.
