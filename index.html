How I work

My Process

01

Discover

Deep dive into the problem space. Understand the stakeholders, the constraints, and what success actually looks like.

02

Research

Explore the landscape. What's been tried? What works? What can we learn from others who've tackled similar challenges?

03

Build & Iterate

Start creating. Test early, learn fast, and refine continuously. The best solutions emerge through iteration, not perfection.

04

Launch & Measure

Ship it, then track what matters. Real impact is measured in outcomes, not outputs. Adjust and optimize based on data.
